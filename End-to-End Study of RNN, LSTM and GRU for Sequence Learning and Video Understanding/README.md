This experiment provides an end-to-end study of sequence learning using RNN, LSTM, and GRU architectures. Students preprocess and represent sequential data, implement the three recurrent models, analyze their training behavior, and evaluate their performance using appropriate classification metrics and visualizations. The experiment also introduces the use of CNN-extracted features with recurrent networks for video understanding and demonstrates sequence-to-sequence learning. Students compare the models based on predictive performance, model complexity, and computational cost, and draw conclusions from the experimental results.
