This experiment investigates the effect of different attention mechanisms and their placement within a pretrained ResNet50 for flower image classification. Students compare channel, spatial, channel–spatial, and self-attention at different network stages, analyze classification performance and computational complexity, and qualitatively study model focus using internal attention maps and Grad-CAM visualizations. Additional attention mechanisms such as SE, ECA, CBAM, and Coordinate Attention may also be explored for extended comparison.
